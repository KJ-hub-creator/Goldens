Story Exploration: Cancer & Diagnoses

Investigating primary endpoints and age at diagnosis in Golden Retrievers

Cancer is the leading health challenge facing Golden Retrievers today, accounting for roughly 75% of deaths in the Morris Animal Foundation cohort. Understanding when these conditions emerge is a vital step toward earlier detection and intervention.

The data reveals distinct age patterns across major diagnoses:

  • Mast Cell Tumors (MCT) and Lymphoma tend to appear earlier in life, with median diagnosis ages around 6.3 and 6.7 years.
  • Osteosarcoma (bone cancer) and Hemangiosarcoma (blood vessel cancer) typically develop later in life, with median diagnosis ages around 7.6 and 7.9 years.
  • Hemangiosarcoma is by far the most frequent cancer diagnosed in the cohort (120 cases), followed closely by Lymphoma (85 cases).
